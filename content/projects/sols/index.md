# SOLS

A Simple Organism Life Simulator on a planet. Enjoy looking at the little creatures spreading and fighting over resources.

![SOLS screenshot](cover.png)

One of my many attempts at making sustainable ecological-evolutionary simulation. The blobs are defined by a multidimensional vector which describes their attributes such as size, speed or energy capacity. But also their behaviour.

Idea of an ecosystem simulator was (boiling) in me for quite a long time. As a child I was playing a game called Gungan Frontier (educational game set in the starwars universe). It was a game from the 90s and I wasn’t able to run it on a more modern machines. When spore came out I believed it was it, an ecosystem simulator. But it wasn’t. It was an ecosystem themed creature maker. After I started my programming career there was a lot of ideas of simulators of various kind.
