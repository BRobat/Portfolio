# Galaxy sim

Quite simple galaxy simulation written in threejs and making use of GPGPU.

![Galaxy sim screenshot](cover.png)

I made it just to see why galaxies often have spiral arms cause most of the explanations I've found on the internet were unconvincing.

It in fact doesn't always produce spiral arms, but it clearly shows that denser groups of stars tend to stay together.
